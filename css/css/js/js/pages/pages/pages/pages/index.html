FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is the waiting list?

The waiting list allows prospective investors to register their interest and provide information about the type of development they are considering.

Does joining the waiting list guarantee land allocation?

No. Registration of interest does not by itself constitute an offer, reservation, allocation, approval or guarantee of land.

What can I propose to build?

Investors may describe residential, commercial, agricultural, tourism, industrial, mixed-use or other development concepts.

Can international investors register?

Prospective investors can submit their interest. Applicable eligibility, ownership, investment and development requirements should be confirmed through the relevant authorized institutions before making investment decisions.

What happens after I submit the form?

Your submission can be reviewed for completeness and relevance. Where applicable, additional information or further engagement may be requested.
